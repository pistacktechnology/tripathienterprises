Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I request event staff?

Earlier requests allow more time for workforce planning and confirmation. However, urgent requirements may also be discussed depending on availability.

Can I hire staff for only one day?

Yes. Staffing can be arranged for short-term requirements, subject to workforce availability and role requirements.

Can I request staff for multiple days?

Yes. Multi-day event staffing can be planned according to the event schedule.

Do you provide supervisors?

Supervisor and coordination support can be included depending on the size and nature of the requirement.

Do you provide staff in Noida and Greater Noida?

Yes, Tripathi Enterprises serves Delhi NCR, including Noida and Greater Noida, subject to availability.

Can I request a specific number of staff?

Yes. Tell us the required number, roles, event dates and location and we can discuss the staffing requirement.

Do you provide female event staff?

Specific workforce requirements can be discussed during the staffing enquiry and are subject to availability.
